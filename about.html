Our Story

Every cup starts with
a good conversation.

Coffee is at the heart of everything we do. For us, it is more than a drink — it is the reason neighbours become regulars and regulars become friends.

How it started

Cosy Cup Cafe opened its doors in Nairobi with one roaster, four tables, and a stubborn belief that great coffee shouldn't be complicated. We started by sourcing single-origin beans directly from smallholder farms, roasting in small batches so every cup tastes intentional.

Years later, the tables have multiplied, but the standard hasn't changed: fresh roast, honest prices, and a team that remembers your order.

What we stand for

Sourced with care

Direct relationships with growers, fair prices, and full transparency on where every bean comes from.

Brewed for connection

A counter designed for lingering — this is where study groups, first dates, and old friends meet.

Perfected by hand

Small-batch roasting and baristas trained to taste-test every batch before it reaches your cup.
